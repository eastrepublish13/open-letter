To Whoever Reads This at Midnight

— left by a stranger

If you are awake and reading this instead of sleeping, I understand. Some nights the quiet gets loud. I don't know you, but I hope whatever kept you up tonight feels smaller by morning. Fold this thought and keep it in your pocket: someone, somewhere, was thinking of a stranger with kindness. That someone was me, tonight, for you.

A Note on Ordinary Tuesdays

— left by a stranger

Nobody writes poems about Tuesdays. But it was on an ordinary Tuesday that you first laughed at something I said, and the whole week changed color after that. I am writing this so that Tuesdays get their due — the unremarkable days are where the real ones happen.
